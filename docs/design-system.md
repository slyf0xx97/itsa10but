# Itsa10but design system: "the sunny climb"

The page is a short walk along a river. A sunny mountain scene is fixed behind the whole page. Content sits on frosted glass panels with soft white edges, two side by side on wide screens, like the translucent cards on a scenic travel site. Text on the ground and on the glass is dark ink.

Reference mood: frosted glass cards over scenery, teal tabs and orange price tags, a trail with pins across terrain, flat painted scenes with tiny figures, tip cards with an illustrated header, segmented progress and a status chip.

## Surfaces

- **Hero points**: the three cards beside the headline are an `ol` (`.hero-points`); each icon tile carries a pixel number badge 1, 2, 3 (`.hero-num`, lime, ink border, display font, `aria-hidden` because the list is already ordered).
- **Hero plate** (`.hero-text` in `app/hero.css`): the heading is the pixel font at `clamp(1.8rem, 3.2vw, 2.5rem)` with the word "but" on a lime marker stroke (no coral, so nothing in the hero reads as an error). The lede is body text (Atkinson Hyperlegible) at `clamp(1.05rem, 1.5vw, 1.2rem)`, line height 1.55, `--text` ink, with the two tooltip words in bold on a dotted `--teal-dark` underline. Under the buttons sits `HeroAgents` (`components/career/hero-agents.tsx`): the four readers as `PixelAgent` sprites in a dashed strip that fills the plate's spare height (7rem, 5.6rem on phones). Four transform layers keep motion calm: the slot swaps places in pairs every 15 to 17 seconds, `.agent` drifts 4px sideways, `.ha-hop` waves now and then and hops on hover (with a name tag), and the sprite bobs (`ui.css`). Four pixel sparkles fade in and out. Motion pauses off screen (an `IntersectionObserver` toggles `.is-off`) and the global reduced-motion rule leaves a still, tidy row with no sparkles. The strip is `aria-hidden` and its sprites are not focusable, because the readers are described in their own cards. "Play sample" is a split button (the caret opens the sample role list) with no caption beside it: only its tooltip (`title`) says "Runs a made-up résumé and job from start to finish."
- **Glass panel** (`.card`, `.panel`, `.tool-card`, `.lu-ribbon`, `.lu-group`, `.hiw-panel`, `.radar-section`, `.qm-card`, `.skill`, pop-overs): `background: linear-gradient(145deg, var(--glass-strong), var(--glass))`, `backdrop-filter: blur(14px) saturate(1.25)`, `1.5px solid var(--glass-edge)`, 18px radius, `box-shadow: var(--glass-shadow), 0 0 0 1px rgb(31 36 48 / 14%), inset 0 1px 0 rgb(255 255 255 / 90%)`. No stripes, no hard dark offset shadow. Where `backdrop-filter` is missing, an `@supports not` fallback makes the panel opaque white at 96% (92% for inner glass).
- **Inner glass** (`.lu-card`, `.hiw-fact`, `.arch-lane`, `.notice`, `details.fold`, `.quests li`, panels inside `.tool-card` and `.hiw`): white 82%, same edge, no blur of its own.
- **Tab / ribbon**: `--teal-dark` block with white pixel-font text and a notched top, used for panel titles.
- **Tag / price chip**: orange rounded tag with ink outline and ink text, a soft orange shadow.
- **Pill** (status, XP): rounded, ink outline, pastel fill; mint = done, yellow = working, coral = gap.
- **Segmented progress**: 3 to 10 equal segments with small gaps, ink outline, `--meadow` or `--orange` fill.
- **Header**: white 92% glass bar with blur, ink brand, orange brand badge, a 2px ink-tinted bottom edge. The two tabs are pixel-bordered buttons set in the reading font (see Header tabs); the bar has two rows from 960px (logo, pill and Agent power, then the tabs).
- **Popups** (`components/popup.tsx`, `app/popup.css`): a native `<dialog>` that closes on Escape and returns focus to the button that opened it. A plain popup (a single tip, the MIT licence) is up to 60rem wide. A wide one (`wide`: the four reader findings, the full tips, company values, news and sources, résumé format tips, the reader details and "Two ways to run") is up to 1100px. Both are 94vw at most and up to 90% of the screen tall, and the body scrolls inside. At 640px and below every popup is a near full-width sheet (the screen minus .75rem, 94% tall).
- Text on the ground is `--text` (or `--text-soft` for hints), with a `0 1px 0 rgb(255 255 255 / 70%)` lift instead of a dark halo. Text on glass is `--ink`. Do not put cream text on the bright ground or on glass.

## The fixed mountain backdrop

One scene sits behind the whole page and stays put while the content scrolls.

- **Scene** (`app/theme.css`, `body::before`): `position: fixed; inset: 0; height: 100lvh; z-index: -1; pointer-events: none` with `url(/assets/scenes/hero-landscape.svg) center bottom / cover no-repeat` over `#a9d8e8`. It is a pseudo-element because `background-attachment: fixed` is ignored on iOS. It never exceeds the viewport, so it adds no horizontal scroll.
- **Fallback colour**: `#a9d8e8`, the top of the scene's sky, set on `html` (and `body` is transparent) so overscroll shows sky, not white.
- **Hero**: transparent, with no image of its own, so the scene is drawn once and does not shift on scroll. The drifting clouds still sit in the hero.
- **Why panels are more opaque**: the scene is busy (mountains, meadow, sky bands), so panels are white 94% (strong) to 88%, inner glass 82%, pop-overs 94% to 98%. The blur and the white edge keep the frosted look. The header and footer are glass plates too, so no text sits bare on the scene.
- **Text** sits on glass in `--ink`. `--text` is only for the rare line that sits outside a panel.
- `hero-landscape.svg` has no bottom fade, because pinned to the viewport it would read as mist over the meadow.
- No elevation numbers or altitude labels.

## Tooltips

`WordTip` (`components/career/word-tip.tsx`, styles in `app/tooltip.css`) is the one tooltip card: used by `<Term>` (entries in `lib/glossary.ts`), the How it works stations and the privacy panel. A rounded card with a 2px ink border, a soft shadow and a small pixel offset, 22rem wide at most (24rem on the map). Top: a coloured header strip with an icon tile and the title in the display font (`tone`: teal, water, coral, orange, lime or leaf; white text on the dark fills, ink on orange and lime). Body: the reading font at 16px. A glossary entry can add `sections` (an icon, an upper-case label and short points with pixel bullets in a tinted block), an `icon` (a `PixelIconName`) and a `tone`. An arrow points at the word and the card flips above it when there is no room below (`data-side`); a card taller than the room scrolls inside its body. It fades in with a 4px rise in 150ms, only under no-preference. Hover, focus, tap, Escape, the hover bridge and keyboard access are unchanged. In the hero lede, "show you how to improve it" opens the "How itsa10but works for you" card (what it does, what it doesn't do, your part).

## Skill rows and the popup

"Skill by skill" (`components/career/skill-rows.tsx`, `app/radar.css`) never changes height. Each skill is one button (`aria-haspopup="dialog"`) showing the name, "Role wants" as High / Medium / Low (1 to 3 dots, `demand` 70 and 40), "You show" as Strong / Some / Weak / None (a 4-step bar, `evidence` 70, 40 and 1) and one verdict chip: Covered (green), Add proof (amber) or Missing (coral). The thresholds and verdict live in `lib/skill-levels.ts` (tested). Rows share one height in a grid, two across from 700px of the block, and scroll inside 36rem past 8 skills. The quotes, the plain "Why this score" line and the exact numbers (behind "How we score") open once in the shared `Popup` (`components/popup.tsx`, native `<dialog>`); they appear nowhere else on the page. Bare percentages are not shown on the row.

## Type

Display: Pixelify Sans (headings, the logo). Body: Atkinson Hyperlegible, which also sets the header tabs, the How it works pill and the Native scan and API boost names (bold, normal letter spacing), because the pixel font read badly at those sizes ("Native scan" looked like "Native soon"). Headings on the ground are ink (`--text`). Minimum body size 16px.

## Motion

Only under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`. Slow parallax drift on clouds, a gentle bob on sprites, quick ease on hover (lift 2px). Nothing blinks.

**Buttons.** `app/buttons.css` (imported last from `globals.css`) is the one hover, press and focus treatment for `button`, `.btn` and `.button`. Under `no-preference` it eases transform, box-shadow and filter over 140ms: hover lifts 2px, grows the hard shadow and adds a little brightness (`brightness(1.06)`); press sinks 1px with a short shadow. ## Scrollbars and overflow cues

`app/scrollbar.css` themes every scrollbar to match the glass look: a 12px pale sky track with a faint inner edge and a pill thumb in `--water-deep` with a white 2px edge (3.5:1 against the track), darker on hover and press, no arrow buttons. The page scrollbar uses the same 12px. Firefox gets `scrollbar-width: thin` and `scrollbar-color` only through an `@supports not selector(::-webkit-scrollbar)` block, because Chrome drops the WebKit styling once those properties are set; so do not add `scrollbar-width` or `scrollbar-color` to component rules. A row that scrolls sideways uses `ScrollHint` (`components/scroll-hint.tsx`, `.sh-*`): white edge fades and round 2.5rem arrow buttons appear only on the side with more content, the arrows scroll 80% of the width (smoothly, instantly with reduced motion), and the first time the row is on screen it nudges 24px and back under no-preference. The arrows are `tabindex="-1"`; the content stays keyboard operable by itself. Today only the Level up tab bar overflows sideways; other rows wrap.
